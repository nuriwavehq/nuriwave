# NuriWave 🇰🇷

### Korea • Journey • Beyond

NuriWave is a Korea-focused digital platform created to help students, future applicants, travelers and Korea enthusiasts discover, understand and explore South Korea in greater depth.

## What NuriWave Covers

- 🎓 Study in Korea
- 🏆 Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)
- 🏫 Korean Universities
- 🗺️ Korean Cities & Regions
- 🎎 Korean Culture & Language
- 🎉 Korean Festivals
- 🧑‍🎓 Student Life in Korea
- 🧮 Interactive Korea Planning Tools
- 📚 Official Korean Resources
- 🤖 NuriWave AI

## Information & Accuracy

NuriWave provides educational and informational content.

Information relating to:

- GKS eligibility
- Scholarship benefits
- University participation
- University admissions
- Immigration
- Health insurance
- Employment
- Festival dates

can change over time.

Users should always verify current information with the relevant official Korean government, university or festival-organizer source before making an application, financial decision or travel plan.
